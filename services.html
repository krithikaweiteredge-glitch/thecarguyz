01 — TECHNIQUE

Foam Cannon Wash

FOAM SOVEREIGN

Snow-foam pre-soak, two-bucket contact wash, pH-neutral chemistry. The grit is lifted and rinsed away before a mitt ever touches your paint — which is exactly why cars that get washed here stop collecting swirl marks.

  • Citrus pre-spray on the lower panels, bug and tar softened first
  • Thick snow foam dwelled for 8–10 minutes, then rinsed top-down
  • Two-bucket contact wash with grit guards and a fresh microfibre mitt
  • Wheels, arches and tyres cleaned with dedicated tools that never touch paint
  • Filtered-water final rinse, blown dry — no towel drag, no water spotting
  • Spray sealant top-up so the next two weeks of rain slide off
  • 45 MIN

  • ANY SIZE

03 — TECHNIQUE

Paint Correction

MIRROR CUTTER

Multi-stage machine polish under 6000K inspection light. Swirls, holograms, water etching and oxidation are cut away in measured passes until the reflection has a hard edge instead of a haze.

  • Paint depth gauged on every panel before a single machine is switched on
  • Defects mapped and photographed under inspection light, then shown to you
  • Cut, refine and finish stages — compounds matched to your clear coat
  • Edges, badges and trim taped; nothing gets burned through
  • Panel wipe with IPA so what you inspect is the paint, not the filler
  • Full report of depth readings handed over with the car
  • 1–2 DAYS
  • from ₹6,999

Paint Protection Film

ARMOUR WEAVE

Self-healing TPU armour, computer-cut to your exact panels. Stone chips, scuffs, trolley rash and road debris stop at the film instead of the paint — and the film heals its own light marks in the sun.

  • Plotter-cut patterns for your exact make, model and year
  • Wrapped edges where the panel allows, so no film line is visible
  • Installed in a sealed, dust-controlled bay — the only way to get it clean
  • Full-front, track pack, or full-body coverage
  • Self-healing topcoat: light swirls disappear with heat
  • Warranty-backed against yellowing, cracking and lifting

05 — TECHNIQUE

Ceramic Coating

THE BARRIER

A 9H silica barrier chemically bonded to freshly corrected paint. Water sheets off, bird lime and road chemicals sit on the coating instead of your clear coat, and the gloss stays locked for years rather than weeks.

  • Applied only over corrected, decontaminated paint — never over defects
  • IR-cured in a sealed bay, then left to harden undisturbed
  • Glass, wheel-face and trim coatings available as add-ons
  • Hydrophobic enough that most road grime rinses off with water alone
  • Annual inspection and maintenance top-up included
  • Layered over PPF for cars that want both
  • 2 DAYS
  • from ₹14,999
  • 3–5 YEARS

07 — TECHNIQUE

Tyre & Rim Care

WHEELS OFF

Wheels are the dirtiest thing on the car and the first thing people look at. Brake dust is corrosive and it bakes on, so the faces come off the car and the barrels and arches get cleaned properly rather than hosed at.

  • Wheels removed so the barrels and arches can actually be reached
  • Iron fallout dissolved, then agitated by hand — no acid on your finish
  • Arches cleaned and dressed, suspension components wiped down
  • Tyres deep-cleaned to lift old dressing, then dressed satin, not shiny
  • Wheel faces coated so the next lot of brake dust rinses straight off
  • Bolts torqued to spec and rechecked before the car leaves
  • 3 HRS
