01What is Paint Protection Film (PPF), and how does it work?

PPF is a nearly invisible, durable film applied to your car’s exterior to protect the paint from scratches, chips, and environmental damage. It acts as a protective barrier, keeping your car looking new while maintaining its original paint finish. The advanced TPU technology also features self-healing properties where light swirls vanish in the sunlight.

02What are the benefits of Ceramic Coating?

Ceramic coating provides a durable, long-lasting layer of protection for your car’s paint. It enhances the shine, repels water and dirt (super-hydrophobic), and shields against UV rays, acid rain, and oxidation, making it easier to clean and keeping your vehicle looking showroom-fresh for years.

03How often should I get my car detailed?

It depends on how frequently you use your vehicle, but we generally recommend getting your car detailed every 4 to 6 months. Regular detailing helps maintain your car’s appearance, protects the paint, and prolongs the life of both interior upholstery and exterior clear coat.

04How long does a car detailing service take?

The time required depends on the type of detailing service you choose. A basic luxury wash and quick interior clean can take 45–60 minutes, while more extensive services like ceramic coating or full interior and exterior rejuvenation take 1 to 2 days, and complete PPF installation takes 2 to 4 days for proper installation and undisturbed curing.

05Can ceramic coating be applied on any car color?

Yes, ceramic coating works on any car color and enhances the natural gloss and optical depth of the paint. It offers equal protection against dirt, UV rays, and water spots while adding an impressive mirror shine across both light and dark shades.

06What’s the difference between a car wash and detailing?

A car wash is a quick cleaning of your vehicle’s exterior. Detailing, on the other hand, involves deep decontamination, machine polishing, scratch removal, and restoring both the interior and exterior of the vehicle to near-new condition. Detailing focuses on protecting and preserving your car’s long-term beauty and resale value.

07Do you offer warranty on PPF & Ceramic Coatings?

Yes. Our Paint Protection Film installations are backed by comprehensive multi-year warranties against yellowing, cracking, bubbling, and lifting. Ceramic coatings come with warranty certificates alongside complimentary periodic check-ups and maintenance advice.
