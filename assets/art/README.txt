FEATURE ARTWORK
===============
figure.png is the illustration shown in "The Standard" section.
Replace it with any image (png or jpg, same name) to change it.

It is masked at the edges so it dissolves into the black background,
so a full illustration with its own dark backdrop works well -
it does not need a transparent cut-out.

Use artwork you own: generated with an image model, commissioned,
or licensed. Artwork depicting a recognisable existing anime character
belongs to its studio even when that particular picture was AI-generated.
