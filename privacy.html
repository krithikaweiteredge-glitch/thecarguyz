This policy explains what information The Car Guyz collects through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have — in plain language, no fine-print games.

Overview

The Car Guyz ("we", "us", "our") runs this website to showcase our car and bike detailing, PPF, and ceramic coating services, and to let visitors book appointments or ask questions. This policy covers what information we collect when you use the site, why we collect it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

When you fill out our booking or contact form, we collect the details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address, and information about your vehicle and the service you're interested in. We do not require you to create an account to use this site.

Like most websites, our server and analytics tools may automatically log basic technical data — browser type, device type, approximate location from IP address, and which pages were viewed — to help us understand how the site is used and to keep it running smoothly.

Cookies

We may use a small number of cookies to remember your preferences and to understand overall traffic patterns on the site. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time; the site will still work, though some conveniences may be lost.

How We Use Your Information

We use the details you submit to respond to your enquiry, schedule your appointment, and follow up about the service booked. We may occasionally use your contact information to share appointment reminders or service updates. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Sharing of Information

We only share your information with trusted service providers who help us operate the business — for example, scheduling or communication tools — and only to the extent needed to deliver our services. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law.

Data Retention & Security

We keep booking and enquiry details only for as long as needed to provide our services and maintain reasonable business records, and we take sensible precautions to protect the information you share with us. No method of storing or transmitting data online is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

Your Choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by reaching out through our Contact page. We will act on reasonable requests promptly, subject to any records we are legally required to keep.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for adults booking vehicle services and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links to Other Sites

Our site may link out to third-party platforms such as Instagram or map services. We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites, so we encourage you to review their own policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our website or services evolve. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach out via our Contact page and we'll be happy to help.
