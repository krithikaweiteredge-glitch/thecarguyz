FAQ

EVERYTHING YOU
WANTED TO ASK

The questions that come up in the bay, answered the way we answer them in person.

01What is Paint Protection Film (PPF), and how does it work?

PPF is a near-invisible self-healing urethane film laid over your paint. Stone chips, scuffs and trolley rash hit the film instead of the clear coat, and light swirls in the film close up on their own in the sun. We plotter-cut it to your exact panels and wrap the edges wherever the panel allows, so there is no visible film line.

02What are the benefits of ceramic coating?

A 9H silica layer bonded to corrected paint. Water sheets off instead of sitting, road film and bird lime land on the coating rather than your clear coat, washing takes half as long, and the gloss stays locked for three to five years rather than the six weeks you get from a wax.

03How often should I get my car detailed?

A maintenance wash every two to three weeks keeps a coated car looking new. A full detail once or twice a year is plenty if the car is protected. If it is not protected and lives outside, expect to correct it roughly once a year — which is exactly the cycle a coating breaks.

04How long does a service take?

An Express wash is about 45 minutes. A full detail is a day. Correction runs one to two days depending on condition, and PPF is two to four days because the film has to be laid and then cured undisturbed. We book the bay for the car, not the clock, so you get a realistic date up front.

05Can ceramic coating be applied on any colour?

Yes — any colour, and over PPF too. Dark colours see the most dramatic change because the defects a coating locks gloss over are far more visible on black and dark blue. White and silver mostly gain the self-cleaning behaviour rather than the shine.

06What is the difference between a car wash and detailing?

A wash removes what is sitting on the paint. Detailing removes what is embedded in it and then corrects the paint underneath — decontamination, machine polishing, protection, and the interior done properly. One takes 45 minutes, the other takes a day, and only one of them changes how the car looks in sunlight.

07Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. PPF is warranty-backed for five years against yellowing, cracking and lifting. Ceramic coatings carry a three to five year warranty depending on the package, with a free annual inspection and maintenance top-up in the calendar.

08Is the quote final?

The quote is confirmed after inspection and before any work begins — never after. Prices scale with vehicle size and paint condition, and if the car needs less than you came in asking for, we will tell you and charge less.
