WHO WE ARE

NINE YEARS
IN THE BAY

Four and a half thousand cars, one rule we have never broken: you get the honest assessment before you get the invoice.

01 — THE SHOP

A BAY BUILT
AROUND THE LIGHT

Most places wash a car. We take it apart optically — inspection light, paint-depth gauge, panel by panel — and we tell you what it actually needs. Sometimes that is less than you came in asking for, and we will say so.

01

Honest assessment

You get the paint-depth readings and the real condition of your car before a single machine is switched on. No mystery packages.

02

Measured correction

We remove defects, not your clear coat. Every pass is deliberate, every pass is logged, and we stop when the paint says stop.

03

Sealed, dust-controlled bay

Film and coating cure in a closed bay with filtered air. It is the difference between a finish that looks right and one that looks right under a torch.

04

Protection that lasts

Warranty-backed film and coating, an annual check-up in the calendar, and a maintenance plan you can actually follow.

02 — THE ARC

HOW A CAR
GETS REBORN

Five stages, in this order, every time. Skipping one is how shops end up polishing grit into paint.

  1. 01

    Inspection

    Paint depth gauged, defects mapped under 6000K light. You get the honest report, not the upsell.

  2. 02

    Decontamination

    Foam, iron fallout dissolve, tar removal and clay. Every embedded particle leaves before anything spins.

  3. 03

    Correction

    Cut, refine, finish. Measured passes only — we remove defects, not your clear coat.

  4. 04

    Protection

    Film laid or coating bonded, then cured in a sealed, dust-controlled bay. The barrier goes up.

  5. 05

    Handover

    Final QC walkaround with you, aftercare kit in the boot, and a maintenance plan that keeps it looking like this.

03 — THE CREW

THE HANDS ON
YOUR PAINT

Small team on purpose. The person who inspects your car is the person who finishes it.

The Lead

Correction & coating

Nine years on the polisher. Reads paint-depth gauges for a living and will talk you out of a second stage if your clear coat cannot take it.

The Wash Crew

Decontamination & interiors
